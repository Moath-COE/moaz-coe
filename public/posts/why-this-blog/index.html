Hello Everyone!
#

I’m Moaz, a university student living in Dhahran. I’m studying Computer Engineering at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM). This is my final year at university, God willing… Yep, four years have passed since I first started! 🙂 My personal field of study and passion is focused on Cloud Computing, IT Infrastructure for websites and applications, and DevOps in general.

Another Personal Blog?
#

Yes, this is a personal blog. And why not?

Personal blogs might not be the most exciting thing for many people, especially in this day and age, but the biggest benefit is usually for the blog owner 😁. So, I won’t pretend this site isn’t just another personal blog. However, I do intend to publish a lot of articles about the tools and technologies I’ve learned—and will learn, Insha’Allah—throughout my university years and beyond. Therefore, I believe this blog will hold some value for specific groups, especially university students, tech enthusiasts, and those interested in computer fields generally. Not only that, but I’m also deeply interested in Finance and Entrepreneurship. My day rarely passes without following global market news and economic events. That’s my love for finance; now, what about entrepreneurship 👀🤔? I plan, Insha’Allah, to write an article series titled “Diary of a Beginner CTO”. This series will cover the business side of my life, including the skills and experience I’ve gained while working as a technical partner for the Chapter-14 platform (don’t worry, we’ll talk about it a lot in the near future!).

A Copy-Paste from ChatGPT 😑?
#

Well, we must address the most trending and controversial topic in our current world, even if it’s just in my very first blog post 🫠. Will I let Artificial Intelligence write my articles and posts for me? 100%, absolutely! Do people still write things themselves in this era?

I’m just kidding! I want this blog to reflect my personality and thoughts as much as possible, and AI-written articles simply cannot express things my way or deliver my ideas the way I intend. Besides, I am convinced that they won’t be as beneficial to the reader as if I wrote them myself, let alone the fact that I would lose the whole meaning of writing and blogging in the first place!

Does this mean that ChatGPT won’t touch the articles on this blog 🤔? Actually, the answer is also no. When AI first emerged and started spreading, I took a hostile stance toward it from day one. I disliked it intensely and decided to stay away from it as much as possible. But as time went on, I realized the importance of a balanced and calm perspective on all life issues—including, of course, the topic of AI and relying on it for work and daily activities. I decided to become more open and accepting, looking for the best way to maximize my benefit from it while minimizing any potential loss from overuse. Based on this, I plan to utilize AI—within the context of writing articles and posts—for correcting grammatical errors and restructuring articles if I find better phrasing. My other intention is to use it for translating articles into English to expand the readership and benefit.

For full transparency with the reader, the publishing process will be as follows:

Idea > Idea Development with AI Assistance > Writing the First Draft in Arabic > Improvement and Proofreading with AI > Translating the Article to English with AI > Final Review > Publishing

How Did I Get Into the Cloud?
#

During my studies, I discovered that what truly excites me is not just writing code, but engineering the systems those codes run on. What really ramped up this interest was that my major focuses more heavily on Hardware rather than Software. This is what drove me to dive deep into the world of Infrastructure, Cloud Computing platforms like AWS and GCP, servers, and their operating systems (the most important being Linux, of course 😉). Specifically, how I can automate the software development and deployment process (CI/CD) using various tools. This passion will be clear in upcoming articles, where I will detail the reasons and methods for using these technologies and my experience with them.

Focus of Topics and Articles
#

Throughout my blogging journey, I intend—by God’s command—to write about several areas and on several levels:

  1. Personal reviews and write-ups about the tools and technologies I’ve learned in Cloud Computing and IT Infrastructure.
  2. Documentation and showcasing of the side projects I’m working on and the daily tools I use.
  3. Articles about my experiences in the business field, sharing my journey in founding and developing Chapter-14 platform.
  4. Personal opinions on economic events and the launch of new technologies and products in the markets. (Apple, I am coming for revenge! 🤣💪)
  5. Some useful topics that helped me in my university life.

Conclusion
#

I want to re-emphasize that the primary goal of this blog is my personal benefit, and I am not seeking to improve society or cause a radical change in the world 🤣. I don’t mean that entirely; in fact, I would be very happy if the articles I plan to publish—Insha’Allah—helped even a small number of readers and made a difference in their daily lives. Undoubtedly, this is one of the desired outcomes of this work.

Finally, don’t forget to follow me on the various social media platforms where I am present so you don’t miss new articles when they are published, God willing. Also, the next article will be about How I Got My First AWS Certification (CCP)! I’ll talk about my experience and share resources to start preparing for this certificate, so stay ready!

Have a great day, everyone.
