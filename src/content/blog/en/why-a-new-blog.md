The new blog is not a replacement for anything, and it is not just another blog among many. It is where I write about what I study and build, so that I understand the subject more clearly. Readers are welcome, but the first person who benefits from this writing is me.

## What I write here

I write about three things: what I study in AI and software engineering, what I build, and what I learn from both.

The posts are not summaries of books or documentation. Each post starts from a real question I am trying to answer, and it covers what I tried and what is still unresolved.

## Why I write for myself first

Writing exposes gaps. I can read a topic and understand every sentence, then try to write it in my own words and find that I cannot justify a basic decision in it. That gap does not show up while I am reading. It shows up when I write.

When I started learning LiveKit, I tried to write about its core concept and could not explain it clearly. While reading, I thought I understood it. The gap only appeared when I tried to put it in my own words. I went back to the fundamentals and deepened my understanding before I wrote anything else about it.

That is why I see writing as part of the study, not something that comes after it.

## How it differs from project pages

Most of what I have published so far is case studies of projects I built or worked on, such as [Hakamony](/en/blog/hakamony/). A case study explains what I built and how I built it. The blog has room for what a case study does not: the questions I have not settled, the concepts I understood late, and the decisions I have reconsidered.

## Who reads it

The first reader is me, some time later, when I need to remember why I chose something.

Then come engineers who build similar systems and want practical explanations with numbers and trade-offs. Technical leads who have to choose between building and buying are another audience. So is anyone who wants to understand what happens behind AI interfaces, without hype.

## What it will not be

- Not a place to teach programming basics to beginners.
- Not a daily news feed.
- I will not publish a post just to keep to a schedule. A post goes up when I have something to add.

## How I will write

- If a post includes code, I have run it.
- If a post includes a number, I will name its source or explain how it was measured.
- If my view changes on a topic I have written about, I will say so and update the date.

If something I write helps you, that is a bonus, and I am glad of it. If you find a mistake, corrections are welcome.
